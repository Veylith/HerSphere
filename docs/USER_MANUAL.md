# User Manual

## Candidate

1. Log in with the candidate demo account or register as a candidate.
2. Use Forgot Password for account recovery and Email Verification when a verification token is issued.
3. Open Candidate Dashboard to view profile strength, career score, applications, bookmarks, and recommended roles.
4. Open Candidate Profile to maintain education, skills, projects, certifications, achievements, and portfolio links.
5. Open Resume Builder to generate a role-ready resume draft from the profile.
6. Open Resume Upload and paste resume text to extract skills, education, projects, certifications, achievements, and experience.
7. Open Jobs or Internships to search, bookmark, and apply.
8. Open AI Analysis to see eligibility, skill gaps, readiness scores, and smart recommendations.
9. Open Learning Roadmap to see courses, projects, certifications, hackathons, and internships.
10. Open Company Reviews to inspect trust scores and submit a review.
11. Open Notifications to track application updates.
12. Open Settings to switch light or dark mode.

## Recruiter

1. Log in with the recruiter demo account or register as a recruiter.
2. Open Recruiter Dashboard to view company hiring analytics.
3. Post a job or internship after company verification.
4. Review AI Candidate Ranking for applicants.
5. Shortlist candidates or move them to interview.
6. Schedule interviews through the API workflow.

## Administrator

1. Log in with the admin demo account.
2. Open Admin Dashboard to monitor users, recruiters, companies, listings, and flagged reviews.
3. Verify pending companies.
4. Approve recruiters through the admin API.
5. Moderate flagged reviews.
6. Review platform reports and audit logs.
